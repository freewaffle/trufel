a(b() + c(d(123, 456), 3) + 4) * 2

1. find deepest call
    a(b() + c(d(123, 456), 3) + 4) * 2
    ^0^1    ^1^2
    so the deepest is d() with weight 2

2. replace these calls with IFC (InlineFunctionCall)
    starting from d()

    collecting arguments:
    d(123, 456)
    I A1   A2

    creating IFC:
    InlineFunctionCall {
        name: d
        args: [[123], [456]]
    }

    replacing entire d():
    a(b() + c(IFC, 3) + 4) * 2

    and recursively...
    > a(IFC + c(IFC, 3) + 4) * 2
    > a(IFC + IFC + 4) * 2
    > IFC * 2
    and now it's ready!
